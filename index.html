CasePoint

A legal-research RAG platform with grounded citations & role-based access.

CasePoint helps legal staff search and reason across case files. Every AI answer is paired with the exact passage and page it came from. The system supports role-based access, document insights, statistical reports, and a choice between OpenAI and a local Ollama model for privacy-sensitive queries.

About

Bio

I build small, considered web tools — currently exploring the intersection of AI and front-end craft.

My focus is on the front-end: HTML, CSS, and the precise bits of JavaScript that make a page feel intentional. I spend most of my time on the basics — typography, spacing, motion — because that's where the perceived quality of a product comes from.

Lately I've been thinking about how AI can support real workflows without getting in the user's way. CasePoint is one expression of that idea: an answer is only useful when you can trace it back to a source.
