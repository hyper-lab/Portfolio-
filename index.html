Featured

CasePoint

A legal-research RAG platform with grounded citations & role-based access.

CasePoint helps legal staff search and reason across case files. Every AI answer is paired with the exact passage and page it came from, so an answer is never a black box. The system supports role-based access (Basic, Advanced, Admin), document insights, statistical reports, and a choice between OpenAI and a local Ollama model for privacy-sensitive queries.

About

I'm a developer who likes clean code, fast experiences, and interactions that feel intentional — small details that make a product feel cared for.

My focus is the front-end: HTML, CSS, and the precise bits of JavaScript that make pages feel alive without becoming brittle. I spend a lot of time on the basics — typography, spacing, motion — because that's where most of the perceived quality comes from.

Lately I've been thinking about how AI can support real workflows without getting in the user's way. CasePoint is one expression of that idea: an answer is only useful when you can trace it back to its source.
